# Keymaker price list (sample)

The only source of list prices for this agent when no canon is present. Sample numbers for a
fictional agency - replace with yours. An invoice line that is not on this list, or not on the deal's
handoff from Sales, is `[NEEDS-AMOUNT]`.

| Offer | What the client gets | Price | Billing |
|-------|----------------------|-------|---------|
| Automation audit | We map what they run (agents, flows, scripts), decide keep / wrap / rebuild, and deliver a readiness report | $2,500 fixed | in full on signature, net 14 |
| Pilot | One department on their own Trinity instance: install, two agents, one real workflow live | $12,000 fixed | in full on signature, net 14 |
| Build | The agency system: departments, shared brain, orchestrator, workspace for their team | from $40,000 | 50% on signature, 50% at go-live; net 14 |
| Operate | We run the instance with them: monitoring, improvements, new playbooks | $2,500 / month | monthly in advance, net 14 |

Rules: no discounts on the audit; a pilot credit applies only within 90 days; a discount up to 10%
on a Pilot or a Build is given as an invoice credit line, never as a lower rate; above 10% the
principal decides.
